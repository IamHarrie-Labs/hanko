# Echo Index: how much crypto "consensus" is one voice repeated?

Generated by `hanko echo-index publish` from the committed `posts.jsonl`. Every number below re-derives from that file, on any machine.

| Echoes found | near-duplicate text: 4 |

**What it says.** On these channels, apparent consensus is almost never one voice repeated: they rewrite stories rather than copy or forward each other. The multi-channel agreement a narrative agent would see here survives the test Hanko applies, with verbatim copies and forwards removed.

**What it found in Hanko.** Before this study, Hanko's echo detector missed every copy listed below. Channels append their own boilerplate (an ad, a 'News | Markets | YouTube' footer), and that padding dropped the Jaccard similarity of a verbatim headline under the 0.6 line, so one headline counted as two voices. Fixed in D-14 by also comparing containment, which padding cannot dilute. Regression test: `tests/test_echo_containment.py`, built on two of these real posts.

**What it does not show.** Rewrites of one press release still count as separate voices: the Swiss spot Bitcoin ETF approval was covered by several of these channels in their own words within hours. For opinions that rule is right; for news it is generous, so echo inflation here is if anything undercounted. And naming the same token is not converging on the same narrative: in a six-hour window, two channels mentioning BTC are usually telling two different stories.

## Method

- Channels: `WatcherGuru`, `cointelegraph`, `TheBlockNews`, `BWEnews`, `DegenerateNews`, `CryptoRankNews`, `foresightnews`, `unfolded`, `crypto`, `bitcoin`, `spotonchain`, `arkhamintelligence`, `cryptoquant_official`, `whale_alert_io`, `binance_announcements`.
- Posts: each channel's public preview at `t.me/s/<channel>`, paged back 14 days, parsed by `TelegramSource.parse()`. Forwards keep their forward marker. Media-only posts carry no text and are skipped.
- Mentions: a fixed list of 53 crypto tickers, detected by cashtag, whole-word ticker or a listed common name. No model and no sentiment: the question is only who named what, when.
- Echoes: `assess_convergence`, the same function `decide()` runs, at the default policy (echo window 90 min, similarity 0.6).
- Fetch log, per channel: `collection.json`. Channels that returned nothing in the window are listed there, not dropped.

Re-derive: `hanko echo-index publish`. Re-collect (new data, new numbers): `hanko echo-index collect --days 14`.
