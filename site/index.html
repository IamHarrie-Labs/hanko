An agent that files receipts.

Hanko reads what crypto traders and news sites are saying, decides whether to act, and seals its reasons before the market answers. Three days later it checks itself in public, wrong calls included.

  • It spots copied posts, so five sites repeating one story count once.
  • Every decision says, in advance, what would prove it wrong.
  • Nothing can be edited later: each run is fingerprinted and timestamped on Bitcoin.

    When it knows less, it bets less.

    Here one safety check could not be reached. No special rule handles this. The missing check lowers Hanko's confidence, and lower confidence means a smaller position.

    The promise is sealed before the answer arrives.

    Changing one word of a sealed decision changes its fingerprint, so any later edit shows.

    Example with made-up data
    When it decidedSealed
    Enter TOKENA
    size 3.28% · confidence 0.66
    ! wrong if the price falls 15% within 3 days
    ! wrong if trading depth drops 30% within 3 days
    ! wrong if fewer than 2 voices still agree in 3 days
    Three days laterGraded
    Enter TOKENA
    x the price fell more than 15%
    + trading depth held up
    + 2 voices still agreed

    One failed condition is enough: the decision is graded as proved wrong even though the other two held.
