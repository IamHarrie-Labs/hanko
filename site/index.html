02 · Missing data shrinks the position

    
Nothing in the rules changed. Only what it knew.

    
No rule says "halve the size if the safety check is unavailable." A gap lowers completeness, completeness lowers quality, and quality sets size — combined as a geometric mean, so one component at zero takes the whole score to zero.

04 · Pre-registration

  
The commitment is sealed before the outcome is known.

  
Move a threshold, a size, or a review date after the fact and the decision_id changes too — a new decision, not an edited one. Hindsight rationalisation becomes structurally impossible. Refusals commit too: a PASS records what would have flipped it.

05 · How it works

  
Four stages, and one boundary that matters.

  
Everything before the boundary may touch the network and fail; everything after it is a pure function of recorded inputs. Determinism and honest handling of missing data aren't features to remember — they're properties the code's shape enforces.


  

    

      
Stage 01

      
Collect

      
X, RSS, Telegram and the seven RYO research tools behind one adapter interface. Every response is stored under the hash of its own bytes.

      
impure · network

    


    

      
Stage 02

      
Interpret

      
Each post becomes a reading: which token, which direction, how strongly, how urgently. Produced once and frozen into the record.

      
impure · model

    


    
PURITY BOUNDARY


    

      
Stage 03

      
Decide

      
Independence check, evidence quality, verdict, size, and the falsifiers the decision commits to. No clock, no network, no model.

      
pure · replayable

    


    

      
Stage 04

      
Review

      
At the pre-registered date, each falsifier is checked against observed facts and graded against its own words.

      
pure · replayable

    

  


  
Interpretation is frozen, not repeated, so replaying a decision proves something specific: the reasoning is reproducible — not that the model would repeat itself, because the model isn't part of the question.

HANKO · THE PERSONAL SEAL

      

      

        
A hanko is the seal you stamp on a document to commit to it. The mark goes on before the outcome is known, and it cannot be taken back.

        
That's what this agent does with every decision: pre-registration is a hanko, hashing the commitment into the decision_id is a hanko. Stamped once at decision time. Stamped again at review — HELD or FALSIFIED, over the original.
