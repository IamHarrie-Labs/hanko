The live ledger

Sealed every three hours. Graded three days later.

A scheduled run reads public Telegram, Bluesky and news voices on four tokens, decides, seals the run with its evidence, anchors it in Bitcoin and commits it to the repository. Seventy-two hours later the same job checks every decision against the conditions it wrote down in advance. This page reads those committed files directly. It shows what changed since the last run first, because that is what you came to check.
