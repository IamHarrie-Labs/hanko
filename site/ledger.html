The live ledger

Every decision, and how it turned out.

A scheduled run reads public Telegram, Bluesky and news voices on four tokens, decides, seals the run with its evidence, anchors it in Bitcoin and commits it to the repository. Seventy-two hours later the same job checks every decision against the conditions it wrote down in advance. This page reads those files directly and starts with what changed since the last run.
